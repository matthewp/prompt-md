A 90s-style guestbook. Tiled background, marquee, visitor counter.

On GET, show a form (name, message) that POSTs to /guestbook, and below it about five past entries.

On POST, the request body is the submitted form. Thank the person by name and show their message at the top of the entries. There is no database, so the older entries can be anything you like, and they will be different next time.
